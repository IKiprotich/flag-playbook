Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Routr ("the app") is a tool for designing and sharing flag football plays. This policy describes what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is built and operated by Ian Kiprotich ("we," "us").

This policy is written to be plain and short because the app is plain and short. There are no user accounts, no third-party servers under our control, and no advertising.

What the app stores on your device

When you use Routr, the app stores the plays, folders, and settings you create directly on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (SwiftData). This includes:

  • Folders and play names you create.
  • Player positions, routes, formations, and notes you draw or type.
  • Your app preferences (such as theme choice and snap-to-yard-lines setting).

This data stays on your device. We do not have access to it.

iCloud sync

Routr does not currently sync anything to iCloud — your plays live on the device you drew them on. A future version may offer iCloud sync so your plays appear on your other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple ID. If that is added and you enable it, your data is stored in your own private iCloud database, managed by Apple's CloudKit service. We do not have access to data stored in your private iCloud database. Apple's privacy policy governs how iCloud handles your data: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing plays

The app lets you share an image of a play through your device's standard share sheet (for example, via Messages, Mail, or AirDrop). When you share a play, you choose where it goes. The image contains only what you drew — the play itself. The app does not transmit shared content to us or to any third party.

Analytics and crash reports

The app collects anonymous usage analytics through TelemetryDeck. This is active in the current version — not a future plan. What is sent is a short list of named events, with no content attached to any of them:

  • A play was created, deleted or duplicated.
  • A folder was created.
  • A route was drawn.
  • A play animation was run.
  • A PDF was exported.
  • Onboarding was completed.

Alongside those events, TelemetryDeck records general device information — device model, iOS version, app version — and an anonymous identifier derived from your device, used only to tell one device apart from another, so that ten events from one coach are not counted as ten coaches. It cannot be reversed to identify you, and we cannot use it to look you up.

What is never sent: the content of your plays, the names you give plays or folders, anything you draw, your name, your email address, your location, or your Apple Account. No advertising or tracking identifier is collected, nothing collected is sold or shared with data brokers, and none of it is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, Apple may share a crash report with us through App Store Connect — but only if you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings. We do not run a crash reporting service of our own. These reports describe what went wrong technically and do not include the content of your plays.

Opting out. Being straight with you: the current version has no in-app switch for turning analytics off, and we intend to add one. Until it exists, you can turn off "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, which stops Apple sharing crash reports with us, and you can email ikiprotichian@gmail.com to ask that analytics associated with your device be deleted.

Purchases

Routr offers optional in-app purchases: Routr Pro as an auto-renewing subscription, and Routr Pro Lifetime as a single non-recurring purchase. Either one unlocks the same features.

The transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your billing address, your name or your email. What the app receives from Apple is a receipt saying that a purchase exists and whether it is still valid — nothing that identifies you personally, and it is read on your device rather than sent anywhere.

We do not store your entitlement on our side, or on yours: each time the app launches it asks Apple again whether you are entitled. That means we hold no purchase record of yours at all. Managing or cancelling a subscription happens in your Apple Account settings, which we have no access to. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Email you send to us

If you contact us using the "Send Feedback" option in the app or by emailing ikiprotichian@gmail.com, we receive whatever you write, your email address, and any device information your email client includes. We use this only to respond to your message and improve the app. We do not add you to mailing lists and do not share your email with anyone else.

Children

Routr is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app does not require an account, does not have social features, and does not collect personal information by design.

Your choices and rights

Because your data is stored on your own device, you have direct control over it:

  • Delete your data: Delete folders or plays inside the app, or delete the app itself to remove all locally stored data.
  • Subscription: Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from Manage Subscription in Routr's own Settings screen.
  • Analytics: See "Opting out" above for what is possible today, including asking us by email to delete analytics associated with your device.

If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data rights (such as the EU under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you have the right to request information about, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Given that we do not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of users, in practice we typically hold no personal data beyond emails you have sent us. To make a request, email ikiprotichian@gmail.com.

Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS device security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We do not maintain our own servers that store your data, which means there is no central database for an attacker to compromise.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. The app links here from its Settings screen and from its purchase screen, so this page is always the current version.
